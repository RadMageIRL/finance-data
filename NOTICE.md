# Provider data in this repository

`pipeline/fixtures/raw/` and `pipeline/fixtures/sources/` hold responses saved from the providers on 2026-11-16, used only as test data. They are not a publication of these datasets, and the MIT License of the code does not apply to them. Each remains under its provider's terms:

|---|---|---|
| Effective Federal Funds Rate (`nyfed-effr-full.json.gz`, `effr-*.json`) | Federal Reserve Bank of New York | https://www.newyorkfed.org/termsofuse. © 2026 Federal Reserve Bank of New York. Content from the New York Fed subject to the Terms of Use at newyorkfed.org. The Effective Federal Funds Rate (EFFR) is subject to the Terms of Use posted at newyorkfed.org. |
| Debt to the Penny (`fiscaldata-*.json*`, `dtp-*.json`) | U.S. Department of the Treasury, Bureau of the Fiscal Service | https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/api-documentation/ |
| H.6 Money Stock Measures (`frb-h6-*`, `h6-*.json`) | Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System | https://www.federalreserve.gov/disclaimer.htm |

Values after the saved data in the fixture scenario are synthetic and exist only to exercise a rule.
